Privacy Policy

Last updated · August 25, 2026

In short

  • We collect only what we need to run the service: your account email, the calls made through Sahaiy (audio and transcripts), and any knowledge documents you upload.
  • Your data is processed and stored on infrastructure hosted in India.
  • We do not sell your data. Ever.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, corrections, or deletion at any time — write to privacy@sahaiy.in.

Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to Sahaiy, an AI voice-call automation service operated by Sahaiy Technologies Pvt. Ltd., India ("Sahaiy", "we", "us"). This policy describes how we handle personal data when you use sahaiy.vercel.app and related services.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address and name (as provided at signup or via Google/GitHub sign-in).
  • Calls: audio recordings and text transcripts of calls made by or with AI agents you configure on Sahaiy, along with call metadata (duration, status, timestamps).
  • Knowledge documents: files and content you upload so that your AI agents can answer questions from them.
  • Usage data: basic technical logs needed to operate and secure the service.

Why we process it

We use this data solely to provide the service: creating and securing your account, running your AI call agents, storing and displaying your call history and analytics, and supporting you when you contact us. We do not use your call data to train models without your explicit consent, and we do not sell or rent personal data to anyone.

Where it lives

Your data is processed and stored on infrastructure hosted in India, operated by us and our infrastructure providers (including our authentication and database provider). We control what data is stored and can delete it on request.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account and call data for as long as your account is active. If you close your account or ask us to delete data, we will delete it within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

Your rights (under India's DPDP Act, 2023)

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is processed.
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your personal data.
  • Grievance redressal — raise a complaint about how your data is handled and have it addressed.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@sahaiy.in. We aim to respond to all requests within 7 working days. If your concern isn't resolved, you may escalate it to the Data Protection Board of India once constituted under the DPDP Act.

Our role regarding callers' data

If you use Sahaiy to make calls to other people, you are responsible for obtaining any consents required from those callers for recording and processing. We act as a processor for that content and delete or return it at your direction.

No certifications claimed

We do not claim any security certifications (such as SOC 2) or registrations beyond what is factually true: we apply industry-standard technical and organisational measures — encryption in transit, access controls, and least-privilege access — to protect your data.
